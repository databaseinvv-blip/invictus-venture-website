Why We Stay Small

No associate handovers. No off-shored drafting. No template work.

Most advisory firms scale by leverage — partners sell, associates deliver. We've resisted that and intend to keep resisting it.

When the founder signs the engagement letter, he reads every email, reviews every draft, and is in the room for every difficult conversation. It limits how many mandates we can accept at a time. We are comfortable with that.

The five people on this page are the five people you work with. Not a sales team in front, a delivery team behind.
